## 7. Tips & tricks — eating healthier AND getting more protein (even >100g)

### Reaching ≥70g the easy way
1. **Anchor every meal with a protein.** Breakfast: eggs, Greek yogurt, or the mango protein smoothie. Lunch/dinner: chicken, paneer, tofu, beans, chickpeas, or dal. Three anchored meals ≈ 65–90g before any supplement.
2. **Schedule your supplements, don't just react.** A Fairlife shake (30g) mid-morning + a Greek yogurt (15g) in the evening is a **+45g/day baseline** on top of meals — that alone nearly meets the target.
3. **Rescue the beige breakfasts.** If the generator gives toast/oat-bar/avocado-toast, add an egg (+6g), a Greek yogurt (+15g), or swap to the smoothie (+31g).

### Pushing to >100g protein
Stacking is how you get there without weird meals:
- **Base:** three anchored meals ≈ **70g**
- **+ Fairlife shake** = **100g**
- **+ 1 Greek yogurt** = **115g**

**Sample 115g day:** Mango Protein Smoothie (31) → Quesadilla (59)… already 90 → add an evening Greek yogurt (15) = 105 → Fairlife as a snack (30) = **135g** if you're training hard, or drop the shake for ~105g.

### Protein boosters that don't change the menu
- Stir **Greek yogurt** into curries/pasta instead of cream (more protein, less saturated fat).
- Add **paneer or tofu cubes** to rice bowls, stir-fries, and veggie dinners (+12–15g).
- Toss **chickpeas or lentils** into salads, soups, and rice bowls (+8–10g).
- **Extra egg** in scrambles/omelettes/fried rice (+6g each).
- **Chia/hemp seeds** into smoothies, oats, yogurt (+5g).
- **Scoop of protein powder** into the smoothie or oats (+20–25g).

### Eating healthier (balance, not restriction)
- **Pair fiber with protein** — you already have dals, beans, chickpeas, quinoa. These keep you full and steady, and they're your best *vegetarian* protein.
- **Balance the fried/heavy American items** (burgers, poutine, nachos, fries) across the week — the generator's variety + cuisine rules already spread these out; add a veg side (grilled zucchini, cheesy broccoli, butter-garlic veggies).
- **Swap cream/mayo for Greek yogurt** in dressings and gravies where you can.
- **Whole fruit over juice**, and use the veg-forward Indian/Mediterranean half of the catalog as your default — reserve restaurant/eat-out nights (already spacing-limited) as treats.
- **Protein-forward, plant-leaning weeks** are the sweet spot here: lots of dal/paneer/bean/chickpea dinners + eggs and yogurt for breakfast + one chicken or fish anchor a few times a week.
